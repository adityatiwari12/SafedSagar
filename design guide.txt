DESIGN DIRECTION
Create IP-SAKTI Sahayak as a modern Government of India / Ministry of Ayush-style public-service portal. The design should feel official, trustworthy, accessible, information-rich and institutional rather than like a commercial AI SaaS product. Take visual inspiration from Indian government websites while keeping the interface original and clearly branded as an SIH prototype.

DESIGN SYSTEM
- Primary: Government-style saffron/orange
- Secondary: Ayurveda-inspired deep green
- Supporting: Navy blue, charcoal, white, light grey
- Background: predominantly white/off-white
- Use thin grey borders, subtle shadows and restrained rounded corners.
- Avoid gradients, glassmorphism, neon colors and excessive animations.
- Use consistent 8px spacing increments.
- Use 12–16px card padding and generous section spacing.
- Icons should be simple, flat, line-based and professional.
- Use subtle Indian/Ayurvedic visual motifs such as leaves, manuscripts, herbs and traditional patterns without making the interface ornamental.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Use Noto Sans as the primary typeface.
- Use Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi and Indian-language content.
- Headings: bold, high-contrast, institutional.
- Body: clean, highly readable, 16–18px.
- Navigation: 14–15px, medium weight.
- Avoid futuristic AI fonts.
- Maintain strong typographic hierarchy using size, weight and spacing rather than excessive colors.

INTERACTION & UX
- Keep animations subtle and purposeful.
- Buttons should have clear hover/focus states.
- Ensure keyboard navigation and accessibility.
- Make citations clickable.
- Use sticky navigation on desktop.
- Make the Ask IP-SAKTI CTA consistently visible.
- Fully responsive for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Prioritize readability and trust over visual effects.

IMPORTANT CODE NOTES
- Build reusable components rather than one large page.
- Create a central design-token system for colors, spacing, typography, borders and shadows.
- Keep content separate from UI components where practical.
- Do not hardcode API keys or sensitive information.
- Use placeholder/demo data clearly where backend functionality is not yet connected.
- Do not fabricate government notifications, legal provisions, statistics or source URLs.
- Do not use copyrighted Government of India branding in a way that implies official ownership or endorsement.
- The landing page should visually resemble a government public-service portal, but IP-SAKTI Sahayak must remain clearly identifiable as the SIH prototype.
- The overall impression should be: TRUST + AYURVEDA + GOVERNMENT INFORMATION PORTAL + SOURCE-GROUNDED AI.
